Revolve turns a sketch around an axis: shafts, wheels, bottles, grooves. **Add** grows material, **Cut** removes it, **Surface** makes a surface body.

## How to use it

1. Draw the profile on one side of the axis. The axis can be a construction line in the sketch, a sketch line, an edge, a reference [axis](axis.md) or an origin axis.
2. Select the sketch and click **Revolve**.
3. If the sketch has no centreline, click the axis in the 3D view or the tree to fill **Axis**.
4. Set the **Angle** (or drag the ring in the 3D view), then click **OK**.

## Good to know

- **Direction 2** revolves the other way too; **Mid Plane** splits the angle evenly on both sides.
- An open profile needs **Thin Feature**, or **Surface** for a surface of revolution.
