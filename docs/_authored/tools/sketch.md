Every sketch-based feature starts here. Pick a plane or a flat face, and FreeCAD's Sketcher opens on it, looking straight at it.

## How to use it

1. Select a plane (an origin plane, a reference [plane](plane.md)) or a flat face of a body.
2. Click **Sketch** (or press <kbd>S</kbd> then <kbd>K</kbd>). The Sketcher opens on it.
3. Draw and constrain your sketch, then click **Close** in the Sketcher's panel.

With nothing selected, **Sketch** shows the part's origin planes and asks you to click one, as SolidWorks does.

[![Sketch with nothing selected: the origin planes show and the panel asks for one](../../assets/shots/plane-prompt.png)](../../assets/shots/plane-prompt.png)

## Good to know

- Sketch edges of existing bodies can be used straight away: the Sketcher's external geometry tools work as usual.
- A sketch tool such as [Extrude](extrude.md) with a plane or face selected starts a sketch on it for you, and opens the feature when you close the sketch.
- See [sketches and sketch features](../../guides/sketch-features.md).
