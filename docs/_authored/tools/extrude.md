Extrude pushes a sketch out into a solid, SolidWorks' Extruded Boss/Base and Extruded Cut in one tool. **Add** grows material, **Cut** removes it, **Surface** makes a surface body.

## How to use it

1. Select a sketch (or click **Extrude** with nothing selected and pick one).
2. Click **Extrude**. A preview shows the result; green adds, red removes.
3. Choose **Add** or **Cut**, an **End condition** (Blind, Through All, Up To Next...) and a **Depth**, or drag the arrow in the 3D view.
4. Click **OK**.

## Good to know

- With a plane or flat face selected instead of a sketch, **Extrude** starts a sketch on it and opens the feature when you close the sketch.
- **Selected Contours** extrudes only the regions of the sketch you click, so one sketch can feed several features.
- **Direction 2** extrudes the other way too, with its own end condition. **Thin Feature** makes a wall of a set thickness from the sketch's lines (open sketches need it).
- With several bodies, **Merge result** decides whether a new solid joins the bodies it touches, and **Feature Scope** which bodies a cut reaches. See [Add, Cut and Surface](../../concepts/add-cut-surface.md).
