Loft blends between two or more profiles: a square base to a round top, a hull through sections. **Add** grows material, **Cut** removes it, **Surface** makes a surface body.

## How to use it

1. Make the profiles: sketches on parallel or angled planes, or faces.
2. Select them in order (or click **Loft** and click them in the 3D view, in order) to fill **Profiles**.
3. Set **Start/End Constraints** if the loft should leave a profile square to it or tangent to a face.
4. Click **OK**.

## Good to know

- The loft passes through **Profiles** in the order they are listed. A point can start or end it, for a pointed end.
- **Guide Curves** steer the shape between profiles. **Close loft** joins the last profile back to the first.
