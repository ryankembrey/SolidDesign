Boundary makes a shape through curves in one direction, or through a network of curves in two directions: SolidWorks' Boundary Boss/Base and Boundary Cut. It gives more control than [Loft](loft.md) where curves meet. **Add** grows material, **Cut** removes it, **Surface** makes a surface body.

## How to use it

1. Click **Boundary**.
2. Click the curves for **Direction 1** in order (sketches, edges, curves).
3. Optionally click curves for **Direction 2**, crossing the first ones: the shape then passes through the whole network.
4. Set **Start/End Constraints** for how it leaves the first and last curves, then click **OK**.
